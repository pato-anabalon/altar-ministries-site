## Platform

web

## Users
1. **New visitor in Auckland**, often a Latin American migrant, looking for a church: when and where it meets, in which language, what to expect, how to get there.
2. **Members**: upcoming events, resources, sermons and the online stream.
3. **Network and allies** (Global): church plants, partners in faith, supporters and donors.

## Product Purpose
Bilingual website for Altar Ministries, a young church in Ponsonby, Auckland (founded 1 April 2023, first initiative of the Nexus Initiative Trust). It exists so a stranger feels invited to an Experience and turns up on Sunday. Success: planned visits, contact enquiries, newsletter sign-ups, online views.

## Positioning
A relational church that calls itself "contracultura": the opposite of a megachurch and of modern ecclesiastical opulence. Familiar and intimate, yet majestic. Gatherings are called **Experiences**, not services, because each one seeks encounter, revelation and transformation. Truly bilingual (EN/ES), home to migrants, local families and new generations.

## Operating Context
- Sunday Experience at St Stephen's Presbyterian Church building, Ponsonby (heritage building: gothic arches, rose window, stained glass). Friday Home Experience, Prayer Experience (Zoom), Community Experience, yearly 21 days of fasting.
- Services are streamed online.
- Community work: church pantry, Christmas dinner, kids' presents, migrant and practical support.
- Content is managed by Nodo (the agency) as code; no CMS.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Site map, phases and stack: `docs/PLAN-IMPLEMENTACION.md`. Open items and placeholders: `docs/PENDIENTES.md`.

- Phase 2 (not at launch): giving, opportunities, our churches, supporters, bookshop.

## Product Principles
1. Invitation before information: every page should make the next step (visit, watch, contact) obvious.
2. Intimate, not institutional: never look or sound like a megachurch.
3. Bilingual by design, not by translation afterthought: both languages are first-class.
4. Truth over polish: no invented people, numbers or words.
5. Majesty through restraint: scale, space and light, not effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Many visitors read English as a second language: plain wording, clear labels. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
